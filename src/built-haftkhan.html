The cast

Pixel characters, drawn and animated for the game. Each labour ends with a boss.

The journey

Zabol, the lion’s thicket, the waterless desert, the dragon, the witch, Olad, Arzhang, and the White Div’s cave.

The islands below are the game’s own maps, rendered by Unity for the website, so the site and the game never drift apart.

Jokes that run through it

Three running gags carry the comedy across all seven labours, and each one pays off.

Rostam falls in love

He sees a beauty, hearts appear, he runs. In Zabol she sends him off to fight first; in the desert she’s a mirage; in the fourth labour she really is the witch. The real one, Tahmineh, waits at the end.

Rostam is hungry

A kebab brings back three hearts, a dizi fills them all, doogh refills water in the desert. In the Shahnameh he really does roast a whole wild ass and fall asleep.

The divs are cowards

They shout “Oh no, it’s Rostam!” when they see him, and every one of them has a last line.

In the game

Illustrated scenes open and close each labour; the play is top-down pixel art with a joystick and a sword button.

Achievements

Fifteen medals, from finishing the prologue untouched to beating the White Div.

How it’s made

A one-person studio needs tools more than it needs time.

Builds from one command

Release settings live in a file; a Unity build tool makes the signed 64-bit and 32-bit packages for Bazaar from it.

News without tracking

The game reads a small file on its own website for announcements and forced updates. No push service, no identifiers.

The website renders itself

Islands and animated characters on haftkhangame.ir come straight from the game’s maps and sprites.
