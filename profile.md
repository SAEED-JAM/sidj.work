## Built on my own

- [Luys Map](https://sidj.work/luys-map.md): Telegram app for outages in Yerevan. Luys Map, built and run by Sid Jameson: every power, water and gas outage in Yerevan on one map, with alerts for the places you save. Six sources, a collector every 15 minutes, four languages.
- [Emlagoo](https://sidj.work/emlagoo.md): Android dictation app for Persian schoolchildren. Emlagoo, built by Sid Jameson: the official textbook audio, aligned word by word and played phrase by phrase for dictation homework. 7.6 hours of audio, 34,361 words, live on Cafe Bazaar.
- [Haft Khan](https://sidj.work/haftkhan.md): A funny game from the Shahnameh. Haft Khan, made by Sid Jameson: a funny action-adventure true to Ferdowsi's Shahnameh, in Unity, live on Cafe Bazaar. Seven labours, fifteen achievements, Persian and English.
- [SafheGardan](https://sidj.work/safhegardan.md): A record player for the phone. SafheGardan, a prototype by Sid Jameson: crates, a needle drop and real crackle on the phone, playing 28,160 public-domain 78 rpm sides. Web, Android and iOS from one codebase.
